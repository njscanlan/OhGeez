Oh Geez privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Oh Geez is a free, open-source project for families of kids with feeding tubes. It has two parts: the Oh Geez app at oh-geez.netlify.app, and the Oh Geez plugin for Claude. This page explains what each one does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

The Oh Geez app

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Feeds, care tasks, supplies, orders and the brief are saved in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer.
  • No accounts, no server, no analytics. The app never sends what you enter to us or anyone else. We can't see it, sell it or share it.
  • Backups are files you control. When you save a backup, the file goes to your device. If you send it to someone, that's your choice.
  • Hosting. The app's files are served by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses and request times. Those logs never include anything you type into the app.
  • Deleting your data. Use Plan > Your data > Erase all data, or clear your browser's data for the site.

The Oh Geez plugin for Claude

  • It's a set of instructions, not a program. The plugin runs no code, calls no services and stores nothing on its own.
  • It reads only what you share. When you paste notes, reports or a backup file into a conversation with Claude, the plugin helps Claude organize and explain them. That information is handled under your agreement with Anthropic and your own Claude settings, not by us.
  • It asks for as little as possible. The plugin tells Claude not to ask for or repeat birth dates, medical record numbers, insurance IDs or home addresses.

Children's information

Oh Geez is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Any information about a child stays on the parent's device or in the parent's own conversation with Claude.

Not medical advice

Oh Geez organizes and explains what a care team prescribes. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
